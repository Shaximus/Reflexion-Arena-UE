POINT 4 — is MACHINE_CONTINUATION_ATTESTED properly gated?
Tested against a COPY under a redirected HOME. The real chain is never written.

  a) genuine receipt, intact chain
     -> MACHINE_CONTINUATION_ATTESTED   (expect MACHINE_CONTINUATION_ATTESTED)

  b) unknown payload hash, intact chain
     -> ORIGIN_UNRESOLVED  reason=no prior receipt commits to these bytes for this session
        note=absence of a receipt is NOT evidence of a human — it is absence of evidence

  c) right payload hash but WRONG session
     -> ORIGIN_UNRESOLVED  reason=no prior receipt commits to these bytes for this session

  d) genuine receipt but chain BROKEN (naive tamper elsewhere)
     -> ORIGIN_UNRESOLVED  reason=chain TAMPERED
        (a valid receipt must NOT attest while the chain is broken)

  e) no chain at all
     -> ORIGIN_UNRESOLVED  reason=chain NO_CHAIN

  f) does the word 'human' ever appear as a VERDICT in attest.py?
     verdict values in source: ['MACHINE_CONTINUATION_ATTESTED', 'ORIGIN_UNRESOLVED']
     any verdict meaning 'human': NONE
