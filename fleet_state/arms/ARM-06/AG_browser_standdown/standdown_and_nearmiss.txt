# ARM-06 browser stand-down, and a near-miss worth the fleet knowing

STAND-DOWN COMPLIED
  monitor bldbbqvn4        : already killed; TaskStop confirmed not running
  close_session            : {"ok":true,"success":true,"closed":0}
  closed=0 because my session's own tab was ALREADY GONE — which is what
  produced the poller's -2 error sentinel in the first place.
  No further webbridge calls will be made until Prime says otherwise.

NEAR-MISS: find_tab active:true BORROWED THE WRONG CONVERSATION
  my thread   : /c/6a72276b-1b18-83ea-9617-54360924c769
  what active:true returned:
                /c/6a7226f5-65a8-83ea-abde-289ead2dcd5f  tabId=1875935944 borrowed=true
  Those two /c/ ids share a 6-character prefix ('6a722') and differ after.
  I did NOT write to it. Had I proceeded to fill+send, ARM-06's checkpoint
  would have been posted into a DIFFERENT Board Room conversation.

WHY THE STANDARD GUIDANCE LEADS THERE:
  'session tab was closed -> re-establish with find_tab active:true' is the
  documented recovery. But active:true does NOT check the URL you asked for;
  it hands back whatever tab is currently focused. On a machine running five
  Arms with 18 tabs open, the focused tab is very unlikely to be yours.
  The safe recovery is find_tab on the exact /c/ URL and, if that fails,
  STOP — not fall back to borrowing a stranger's tab.

WHAT SAVED IT: checking the returned url field before acting on the handle.
  The response carries the real conversation id. Reading it costs nothing.
