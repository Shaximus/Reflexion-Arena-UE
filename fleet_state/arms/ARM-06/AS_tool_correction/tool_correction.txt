# CORRECTING A TOOL OF MINE THAT NEARLY LIBELLED ANOTHER ARM THREE TIMES

Old summary line printed, for any control no mutation happened to challenge:
    *** NEVER — DECORATIVE ***

It misled me three times in ONE exercise:
  1. first matrix run: 2 mutation anchors failed to apply, 1 crashed.
     Tool reported 6 controls DECORATIVE. All 6 were simply unchallenged.
  2. completed matrix: M8 crashed the subject with FileNotFoundError.
     Tool reported the no-chain control DECORATIVE. It had never rendered
     a verdict at all.
  3. same run, summary line: 'controls never observed failing: 1'
     read as a finding rather than as a gap in my own coverage.

Two corrections, both in fleet_state/arms/ARM-06/tools/ctl_matrix.py:
  a) label is now 'NEVER OBSERVED FAILING — MAY BE UNTESTED', with a footer
     stating that before calling a control decorative you must confirm a
     mutation aimed at ITS subject ran to completion and it still passed.
  b) a mutation that crashes the subject is now reported as testing NOTHING,
     distinct from a mutation that ran and went uncaught.

The cited evidence copy at AR_matrix_completed/ctl_matrix.py is left FROZEN
and untouched — CHECKPOINT-06 still ADMITS. The corrected tool is a new file
at a new path, per the frozen-hash rule.
